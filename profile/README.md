We bring AI research and chip engineering together to build agentic workflows for analog and digital integrated circuit design.

我们结合 AI 研究与芯片工程经验，探索模拟与数字集成电路设计的智能工作流。

## Research · 研究成果

- **[AnalogLayoutWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16991)** · 2026-11-12 — A benchmark for agentic analog layout design with independent physical, electrical, and layout-quality evaluation. Evaluation code is coming soon.
- **[Industrial Chip Floorplanning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12048)** · 2026-11-09 — A framework and empirical study of AI agents for industrial-level chip floorplanning.
- **[Agent-Orchestration in Autonomous Chip Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14035)** · 2026-08-14 — Perspectives and methodology for coordinating agents in chip design.
